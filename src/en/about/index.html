The story of Tzes

A tattoo studio in Heraklion for people who want something with style, designed just for them.

It all starts on paper

At Tzes Tattoo every design is born on our table: first in pencil, then in ink, until it looks like what you have in mind.

We work in fine line, blackwork, dotwork and traditional, and we love designs that carry a story.
